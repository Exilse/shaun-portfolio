My story

In 2022, I thought I was going to become a video game designer. I graduated high school that same year, and after my studies concluded I had a few short months to figure out whether that was the career I wanted to pursue.

In 2023, I took a general programming course to strengthen my programming fundamentals before enrolling in university.

I hated that course.

Feeling properly lost about what to pursue, I spent the rest of that year looking back at what I enjoyed as a kid, which brought me back to something I had once loved, video editing.

That led me down a rabbit hole over the next three years, improving on a craft I first started as a child. It has taken me down paths in the social media space that the 2022 version of me almost certainly couldn't have imagined.

I'd like to thank everyone who has given me the opportunities I've had so far. None of my progress toward this career would have happened without people willing to give me a chance. I hope to keep building and creating useful things for the people around me, now and into the future.
